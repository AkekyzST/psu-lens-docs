# Login with PSU Passport

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/v2-login.png" alt=""><figcaption><p>The PSU Assessment Lens front page and the PSU Passport sign-in button</p></figcaption></figure>

## Getting started

Getting in is simple: press the **PSU Passport** button on the front page. The system takes you to Prince of Songkla University's central sign-in page (PSU Passport). Once your identity is confirmed, you are returned to PSU Assessment Lens automatically. There is nothing else to set up.

## Which account do I use?

The system accepts **PSU Passport** accounts only, so there is no separate registration, and Assessment Lens never stores your password. Use the same account you already use for other university services.

* **Students** sign in with your student ID, or your student email (`studentID@email.psu.ac.th` or `studentID@psu.ac.th`, depending on your year of entry)
* **Lecturers and staff** sign in with your PSU Passport ID or staff email (`PassportID@psu.ac.th`)

{% hint style="info" %}
**Forgot your PSU Passport password?** No problem — reset it yourself at [onepassport.psu.ac.th](https://onepassport.psu.ac.th), or use the "Forgot Password" link on the front page. If you still cannot sign in, contact the computer centre of your campus.
{% endhint %}

{% hint style="info" %}
Set the screen up the way you like it — on the front page and in the top bar you can switch between **light and dark mode** and change the **language (Thai/English)** at any time.
{% endhint %}

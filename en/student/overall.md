# Homepage

After you sign in, the system takes you to the **Homepage**, which gathers everything you need to know about this semester's evaluations in one place. One glance tells you what is still left to do.

## Progress bar

At the very top is a welcome bar that greets you by name and shows your role (Student), your faculty and campus, and a reminder that **your name is never shown with your answers**.

Below it is the progress summary, so you can see at a glance:

* how many courses you have evaluated out of the total, with a progress bar to make it visual
* which course closes for evaluation soonest, so you don't miss it
* a **"Start evaluating"** button that takes you straight to your courses

Once every course is evaluated, this bar turns into a thank-you message.

## Course evaluation summary

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/v2-student-home.png" alt=""><figcaption><p>The student homepage: progress bar, stat chips, evaluation summary and recent submissions</p></figcaption></figure>

The system counts how many courses you need to evaluate and how many you have finished, shows a few of them as examples, and displays stat chips summarising your courses by status: Registered courses, Open for evaluation, Outside period, and Evaluated.

To see every course, press **"All courses"** or open **"Enrolled Courses"** in the left-hand menu.

## Recent submissions, how it works, and help

Three more helpers sit at the bottom of the homepage.

* **Recent submissions** — what you have just submitted, with a link to your full [Evaluation History](history.md)
* **How evaluation works** — the whole process in 3 short steps
* **Need help?** — links to this manual and to staff contact details, in case you get stuck

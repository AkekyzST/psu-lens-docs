# FAQ

The questions students ask most, with short answers.

### Are my answers confidential?

Absolutely. Every evaluation is anonymous. Lecturers and faculties only ever see combined results and have no way of knowing who wrote what, so you can answer honestly.

***

### I signed in but a course I'm enrolled in isn't listed. What do I do?

Contact the faculty officer responsible for that course, or the system administrator, and they will check your enrolment data and sort it out.

***

### I already submitted a form and want to change it. Is that possible?

You cannot change it yourself once it is submitted. If a change is genuinely needed, contact the faculty officer responsible for that course, or the system administrator, and ask them to "return" the form so you can fill it in again.
